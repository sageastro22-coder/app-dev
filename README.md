# 🎬 My Favorite Series: *My Hero Academia*

---

## 🌟 Overview
**My Hero Academia** is a Japanese anime series based on the manga by *Kohei Horikoshi*.  
It tells the story of a young boy named **Izuku Midoriya**, who dreams of becoming a hero in a world where almost everyone has superpowers called **Quirks**.

---

## 🦸‍♂️ Main Characters
- **Izuku Midoriya (Deku)** – The main character who inherits the power *One For All*.
- **Katsuki Bakugo** – Deku’s childhood friend and rival.
- **All Might** – The Symbol of Peace and Deku’s mentor.
- **Shoto Todoroki** – A powerful hero with both ice and fire abilities.

---

## 💥 Why I Love It
> The story teaches that even without natural talent, anyone can become great through effort and determination.  
> It’s full of action, emotion, and powerful lessons about courage and friendship.

---

## 🎯 My Favorite Episodes
1. **Season 1, Episode 4:** “Start Line” – When Deku proves his bravery for the first time.  
2. **Season 3, Episode 23:** “Deku vs. Kacchan” – Their emotional battle shows real growth.  
3. **Season 6, Episode 25:** “No Man Is an Island” – A great ending showing Deku’s leadership.
